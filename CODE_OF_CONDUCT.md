# Code of conduct

agentQuake is a small project, and we want everyone who uses or builds it
to feel welcome, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good intent. Most mistakes are misunderstandings.
- Critique code and ideas, never people.
- Be patient with newcomers. Everyone asked a first question once.
- Accept that maintainers may say no to a change, and explain why when
  they do.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, including repeated self-promotion in issues and discussions.

## Enforcement

These rules apply in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere you
represent the project.

Report problems privately to the maintainer, [saeedallam](https://github.com/saeedallam),
using the contact details on their GitHub profile.
Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may edit or remove
comments, lock threads, or block people who break these rules.

This code is inspired by the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
